Company Profile

Delivering Maritime & Freight Reliability Since 2010

Alpha Global Shipping & Logistics (www.alphagsl.com) has grown from a regional logistics operator into an international ocean and air freight forwarder operating across key international trade lanes.

We combine advanced technology, direct vessel operator contracts, and hands-on port clearance expertise to deliver cost-effective and timely cargo transportation worldwide.

Our Mission

To provide seamless, transparent, and efficient freight transportation and logistics solutions that empower global trade and give businesses peace of mind.

Our Vision

To be recognized as the world's most dependable, technology-driven logistics provider, connecting continents through modern logistics infrastructure.

Our Services & Solutions

Complete end-to-end global logistics tailored to manufacturers, exporters, and importers.
